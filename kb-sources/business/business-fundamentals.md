## Business fundamentals for product teams
Value proposition: who, what pain, why now, why you. Test the riskiest assumption first; the
riskiest assumption is rarely technical. Business model in one page: segment, value prop, channels,
revenue streams, cost structure, key metrics (BMC-lite).

## Metrics and viability
Pick ONE north-star metric that equals delivered value, plus guardrail metrics. Unit economics:
CAC vs LTV understood before scaling spend. Pricing: anchor on value delivered, not cost; start
with the simplest scheme that can evolve (flat/usage/tier). Distribution beats product for early
growth: name the channel you will actually use.

## Decisions
Write decisions with their reversible/irreversible classification: reversible = decide fast,
irreversible = deliberate and get explicit sign-off. Kill criteria agreed in advance for anything
experimental.
