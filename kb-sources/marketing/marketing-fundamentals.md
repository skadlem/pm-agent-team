## Positioning and messaging
Positioning formula: for <audience> who <situation>, <product> is a <category> that <key benefit>,
unlike <alternative>, we <differentiator>. Messaging hierarchy: one core message, three supporting
proof points, evidence for each. Speak the audience's language, feature-benefit pairs, concrete
numbers over adjectives.

## Channels and launch
Choose 1-2 channels where the audience already is; depth beats breadth early. Funnel per channel:
awareness -> interest -> conversion -> retention, with the metric for each stage. Launch checklist:
landing page, demo/screenshots, social proof, analytics wired, feedback loop live. Content: solve
searched problems (SEO) or entertain where the audience scrolls (social), never generic announcements.

## Measurement
Define success per channel before spending effort: signups, activation rate, or engagement.
Weekly review: what worked, kill/keep/double. Brand consistency: same voice, same visual identity
across every touchpoint.
